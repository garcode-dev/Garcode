Pastor Shalom Gar — Founder, CEO & Chairman

### The Story Behind the Name
The name "Garcode" was inspired by something we see on products used literally every day- The Barcode. It is simple yet valuable for connecting us to information quite effectively.

By removing the "bar" from "barcode" and replacing it with the surname of our Founder & CEO, Rev Shalom Gar, we make a simple statement - This is the kind of technology we are committed to building.

### Our Founding Philosophy
**Don't only teach skills. Shape leaders.** Skills get people hired. Leadership builds economies. Everything we teach is meant to produce people who will lead Africa's transformation, not just take part in it.
**Build with Africa, not just for Africa.** We design culturally aware solutions and work as a collaborative ally, because the people closest to a problem should help shape its solution.
**Learning should lead to earning.** We teach by building real projects, with real mentors, so learners leave with proof of what they can do.
**Every enterprise can thrive in the digital age.** We are committed to collaboration and inclusivity, and to unlocking the potential of every organisation we serve.

### Our Mission
To raise a digitally empowered generation that leads Africa's transformation through technology, creativity and innovation.
